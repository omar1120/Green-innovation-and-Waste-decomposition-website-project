Waste Management

Waste Management is the process of collecting, transporting, processing, recycling, and disposing of waste materials in a safe and environmentally friendly way. It aims to reduce the negative impact of waste on human health and the environment.

  1. Protects the Environment
    Reduces pollution in air, water, and soil.
  2. Promotes Recycling
    Saves natural resources by reusing materials like plastic, paper, and metal.
  3. Economic Benefits
    Creates jobs and reduces costs of cleaning pollution.
  4. Improves Public Health
    Prevents the spread of diseases caused by waste.
  5. Reduces Climate Change
    Proper waste handling lowers harmful greenhouse gas emissions.

  1. Environmental Pollution
    Waste accumulates in landfills and natural areas, harming ecosystems.
  2. Air Pollution
    Burning waste releases toxic gases that damage health.
  3. Water Contamination
    Waste pollutes rivers and oceans, killing marine life.
  4. Health Problems
    Causes diseases, infections, and respiratory issues.
  5. Global Warming
    Landfills produce methane, a strong greenhouse gas.

Effective waste management is essential for a clean environment, healthy communities, and a sustainable future.
